### Climate risk · Mikhail Serikov

How weather and climate extremes damage infrastructure and the economy, and what information and adaptation are worth.
Self-directed research studies on public data by [Mikhail Serikov](https://github.com/michaelserikov) (M.Sc. Ocean and Climate Physics, Universität Hamburg). Each README states what is observed, what is assumed and how the results were checked.

#### Infrastructure under extremes
- **[de-storm-grid-risk](https://github.com/serikov-climate-risk/de-storm-grid-risk)** (flagship): windstorms on the German transmission grid, correlated line failures, DC power flow load shedding and the value of information for reinforcement (stochastic MILP, Benders)
- [gm-transit-climate-risk](https://github.com/serikov-climate-risk/gm-transit-climate-risk): surface water floods and heat on the Greater Manchester bus and tram network, mode choice logit and adaptation sites under climate uncertainty (stochastic MILP, minimax regret, EVPI)

#### Hazard, loss and risk transfer
- [de-windstorm-risk](https://github.com/serikov-climate-risk/de-windstorm-risk): German windstorm loss index, extreme value statistics, CLIMADA calibration, parametric trigger basis risk
- [storm-uncertainty-eu](https://github.com/serikov-climate-risk/storm-uncertainty-eu): windstorm and compound wind-rain uncertainty over Western Europe on ERA5, extremes with NAO and East Atlantic covariates, stochastic event set and importance resampling of loss models

#### Climate economics
- [climate-damage-panel](https://github.com/serikov-climate-risk/climate-damage-panel): regional damage function for weather extremes (fixed effects panel, double machine learning)

**Related:** power systems under climate change in [serikov-energy-systems](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems) (PyPSA weather years, stress storylines, adequacy)
